## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Articulated suspense and outcome reactions
Execution SHALL use a connected shoulder, upper arm, elbow, forearm, wrist/hand and revolver hierarchy. Both results SHALL share a locked 5.7-second anticipation, twice the previous wait, with synchronized generated breathing, mechanical cues, heartbeat and near-silence before reveal. Reduced motion SHALL simplify movements while preserving the same wait. Recovery of an already committed pending result SHALL reveal that saved result without consuming another shot.

#### Scenario: Contrasting outcomes
- **WHEN** the predetermined shot is revealed
- **THEN** a blank clicks and visibly relaxes, while fatal impact synchronizes flash, camera impulse, red/black dimming, recoil and stylized collapse with muffled/ringing aftermath; readable named result feedback precedes automatic return.

#### Scenario: Blank or fatal trigger
- **WHEN** a player presses an enabled FIRE control
- **THEN** the result is persisted exactly once, stays hidden through 5.65 seconds, and is revealed at approximately 5.7 seconds for either outcome.

#### Scenario: Reload during anticipation
- **WHEN** the page reloads while a shot is pending
- **THEN** the same committed result is recovered and returning to the table does not consume another chamber.
